September 2026. Its SHA-256 is
`51d2ee2e84385d5a417fb1114b3adb060827abbc25a99b54035dc04a9f398d58`.

directory, and the bibliography `14sccg_elsa`. Those companion files were not
present in the recovered archive, so this directory is an archival source

remains the reference for the published eight-page content.

The repository's recovered raster figures and media are documented separately.
They must not be silently substituted into this archival source, because the
source may represent a nearby author version rather than the exact version of
record.
