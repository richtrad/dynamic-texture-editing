# Historical video companions

This directory contains four author-archive videos that have a documented relation to the published **Dynamic Texture Editing** paper. The files are preserved byte-for-byte from the downloaded archive copies; their SHA-256 values and technical metadata are in [`media-manifest.json`](media-manifest.json).

| File | Relation |
| --- | --- |
| [`time.mpg`](media/time.mpg) | Figure 9 time-shift example; the video frame is titled “Enlarged texture (time shift)”. |
| [`grass20.mpg`](media/grass20.mpg) | Table 2, `Grass2`; frame matching confirms the recovered raster. |
| [`fence_full0.mpg`](media/fence_full0.mpg) | Table 2, `Fence`; frame matching confirms the recovered raster. |
| [`no3.mpg`](media/no3.mpg) | Table 2, `Tree in the rain`, arrangement no. 3; frame matching confirms the recovered raster. |

The paper's LaTeX does not name video files, so this is a provenance companion rather than a claim that these are the original encoded masters. `no2.mpg` and `ladvi_full.mpg` remain in the private recovery audit because their exact figure mapping is unresolved. The damaged-grass video mentioned in Figure 10 is not included yet.

The videos come from the author's private archive and are published here as historical research companions. Please cite the paper and preserve the file names and hashes when redistributing them.
